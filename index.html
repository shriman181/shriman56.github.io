The result? Defects are found late. Failures happen fast. And no city has the budget to change it — until now.

The Platform

From flight to finding in hours, not weeks.

01
Drone Deploy
A drone flight is scheduled or triggered — no road closures, no rope teams. Data captured at millimeter resolution with RGB, thermal, and LiDAR sensors.
02
Ingest & Process
Raw imagery and sensor streams are ingested into Navella's cloud pipeline, stitched into 3D point clouds and orthomosaic maps automatically.
03
AI Detection
Computer vision models trained on 2M+ infrastructure defect samples classify cracks, spalling, corrosion, and deformation with confidence scores and GPS coordinates.
04
Prescriptive Report
Engineers receive a prioritized risk report: severity scores, historical change tracking, and recommended maintenance actions — integrated with existing GIS workflows.

Detection Capabilities

Trained to see what humans miss.

Micro-crack detection
Hair-width fractures as fine as 0.2mm are flagged and geo-tagged — far below the threshold of naked-eye inspection.
Corrosion & rust mapping
Multi-spectral analysis identifies surface corrosion, oxidation depth, and material degradation on steel and concrete structures.
Structural deformation
LiDAR-based point cloud comparison tracks millimeter-scale displacement, sag, or warping relative to the baseline model.
Spalling & surface loss
Concrete spalling, delamination, and surface erosion are measured by area and depth, mapped to exact structural coordinates.
Thermal anomalies
Infrared sensors detect subsurface voids, water ingress, and thermal bridges in pipeline coatings and dam faces.
Vegetation & erosion risk
Vegetation overgrowth on dam faces, scour around bridge piers, and soil erosion near pipelines detected before they accelerate damage.

The Founder

AG
Ashwar Gupta
Founder & CEO, Navella

Infrastructure doesn't fail suddenly. It fails slowly, and invisibly.

Ashwar Gupta founded Navella after years working at the intersection of structural engineering and applied machine learning. He watched inspection teams spend days on rope access — gathering data that would take weeks more to analyze — and knew there had to be a smarter way.

The problem wasn't lack of data. It was lack of intelligence. Drones were already flying over bridges and dams. What was missing was an AI layer that could interpret what the imagery was actually showing — at the speed that maintenance decisions require.

Navella is his answer to the $47B annual maintenance gap eating through the world's public infrastructure. By combining drone hardware partnerships with proprietary defect detection models trained on millions of structural samples, Navella delivers what city engineers have always needed: a system that knows where to look, what to find, and what to do about it.
